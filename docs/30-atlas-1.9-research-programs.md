# ATLAS 1.9 — Initial World Research Programs

ATLAS 1.9 begins with four explicit possible-world programs.

## 1. Multiscale Semantic Image Codec

Competing worlds:
- current codecs remain Pareto-optimal;
- semantic/multiscale representation yields selective gains;
- apparent gains are dataset/metric overfit.

Minimal tests:
- public rate-distortion benchmark;
- blinded perceptual comparison.

Promotion condition:
no claim of superiority over JXL/AVIF/WebP without reproducible curves and blind evaluation.

## 2. Space Anomaly Fusion

Competing worlds:
- sensor/processing artifact;
- real ordinary phenomenon;
- residual unknown mechanism.

Minimal tests:
- independent cross-sensor persistence;
- calibration/reference-frame reconstruction.

Rule:
unknown does not mean extraordinary; residual status is maintained until a mechanism is discriminated.

## 3. Multimodal Physiology

Competing worlds:
- no added information beyond validated modalities;
- reproducible complementary signal exists;
- apparent signal is leakage/confounding.

Minimal test:
blinded held-out validation.

This program remains human-approval, ethics and medical-validation gated.
No diagnostic claim is permitted from exploratory signal alone.

## 4. Ancient Knowledge / Cross-Corpus Residuals

Competing worlds:
- convergent cultural evolution;
- documented cultural/textual transmission;
- unresolved residual after controls.

Minimal tests:
- source chronology/dependency reconstruction;
- independent archaeological residual test.

Rule:
mythic resemblance is never sufficient evidence for historical contact or extraordinary mechanism.

## Research loop

program
-> world ensemble
-> minimal discriminant experiment
-> posterior update
-> reality gap
-> mechanism extraction
-> technology seed
-> ministry review
-> RIA
-> sandbox
-> outcome
-> knowledge assimilation.
