## Dimensions

Physics:
- 3 spatial + time in ordinary experience;
- extra dimensions remain theoretical;
- collider searches look for observable residuals such as heavy Kaluza-Klein-like states or missing energy.

Data science:
latent dimensions and manifolds.

Metaphysics:
stored as hypothesis/tradition.

ATLAS rule:
same word does not imply same ontology.

## Cross-domain invention principle

Source -> functional abstraction -> physics/math equivalent -> ministry mapping -> experiment.

Example:

Wakandan vibranium
-> absorb/manage mechanical energy
-> metamaterial/damping/structural battery research
-> Science Frontiers + Creation/Design + Math
-> material simulation/prototype/benchmark.

Flower-of-Life torus
-> rotational closed-flow geometry
-> toroidal field/flow model
-> Math + Science
-> compare to ordinary toroidal systems before any consciousness interpretation.

"Seven heavens"
-> layered-state representation
-> hierarchical latent-state model
-> Math + Research
-> benchmark against flat models.

Atlantis collapse
-> coupled infrastructure/social failure
-> resilience simulation
-> Architecture + Governance + Research
-> historical backtests.

## Prohibition

No ancient, scriptural, fictional or esoteric source is allowed to bypass the evidence gate.

Their role is to expand hypothesis space, not lower standards of proof.
